LightRead

Read & Learn · 轻读

LightRead is a reader for learning English and Chinese from real articles: news, culture, science, everyday stories and graded readers. Articles are saved for offline reading; tap any word to look it up, see the sentence translated, highlight, write with Apple Pencil and build a vocabulary list you can export to Anki.

轻读是一款用真实文章学英文、学中文的阅读应用：新闻、文化、科技、日常和分级读物。文章可离线阅读；长按查词、整句翻译、高亮、用 Apple Pencil 书写，并把生词导出到 Anki。
