Privacy Policy

LightRead · Last updated 11 November 2026

LightRead does not require an account and the developer does not run any server that receives your data. This policy explains what stays on your device and what is sent to third parties.

Stored on your device

Downloaded articles and pictures, your highlights and handwriting, your vocabulary list and your settings are stored only on your device. They are deleted when you delete the app. Cached articles older than 14 days are removed automatically, except those you annotated.

Content sources

The app downloads articles directly from the publishers' public websites and feeds (for example Voice of America, NASA, the Library of Congress, Wikipedia). Those sites receive the usual web request information, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Look-up and translation

When you look up a word or translate a sentence or paragraph, that text is sent to Google Translate (and, if it fails, to MyMemory) to get the translation. Only the selected text and the language pair are sent. Look-ups of English words for Chinese readers use a dictionary built into the app and are not sent anywhere.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when asked), IP address, coarse location derived from it, device and usage information, and ad interaction data, to show and measure ads. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA and UK) you are asked first and can change your choice. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Subscription

Premium subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple. The developer receives no payment details. You can cancel in your Apple account settings at any time; premium members see no ads.

Children

LightRead is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue. Changes to this policy are posted on this page.

隐私政策

轻读 LightRead · 最后更新：2026 年 11 月 11 日

轻读不需要注册账号，开发者也没有运行任何接收你数据的服务器。本政策说明哪些数据留在你的设备上，哪些会发送给第三方。

保存在设备上的数据

下载的文章和图片、你的高亮和手写笔迹、生词本以及各项设置，只保存在你的设备上，删除 App 时一并删除。超过 14 天的缓存文章会自动清理，有批注的除外。

内容来源

App 直接从各出版方的公开网站和订阅源（例如美国之音、NASA、美国国会图书馆、维基百科）下载文章。这些网站会按照各自的隐私政策收到一般的网络请求信息，例如你的 IP 地址。

查词与翻译

查词或翻译句子、段落时，所选文字会发送到 Google 翻译（失败时发送到 MyMemory）以获取译文，只发送所选文字和语言方向。中文用户查英文单词使用 App 内置词典，不会发送到任何地方。

广告

免费版显示 Google AdMob 广告。为展示和衡量广告，Google 可能收集设备的广告标识符（仅在你同意跟踪时）、IP 地址及由此推断的大致位置、设备和使用信息以及广告互动数据。在需要征得同意的地区（如欧洲经济区和英国），App 会先征求你的同意，你也可以随时更改。详见 Google 如何使用来自合作伙伴应用的信息。

订阅

会员订阅由 Apple 销售和管理，开发者不会收到任何付款信息。你可以随时在 Apple 账户设置中取消订阅；会员不显示任何广告。

儿童

轻读不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集他们的个人信息。

联系方式

对本政策有疑问，请提交问题。本政策如有变更，将在本页公布。
