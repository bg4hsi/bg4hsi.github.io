FAQ · 常见问题

How do I look up a word? · 怎么查词？

Long-press a word, then choose “Look Up / Translate”. The word is saved to your Words list. · 长按单词，选「查词 / 翻译」，查过的词会自动收进生词本。

Reading offline · 离线阅读

Tap the download button to save the current topic’s articles and pictures. · 点下载按钮，把当前主题的文章和图片保存到手机上。

Remove ads, restore or cancel Premium · 去广告、恢复或取消会员

Tap the crown to subscribe or restore purchases. To cancel, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions. · 点皇冠订阅或恢复购买；取消订阅请到「设置 → 你的名字 → 订阅」。

Export to Anki · 导出到 Anki

Open Words and tap Export; import the file into Anki as tab-separated text. · 打开生词本点「导出」，在 Anki 中按制表符分隔文本导入。
